One image summing up the world news of Wednesday 18 November 2026, drawn
as a wide editorial illustration, **"APEC Dawn"**: a dawn skyline of
Shenzhen with the flags of China, the United States and Russia flying over
a leaders' negotiation table, for the APEC summit opening in Shenzhen
(Trump in Manila for Quad talks; Xi meets Canada's Carney). Four captioned
mini-scenes below cover the Gulf drone incidents (Bahrain all-clear, Kuwait
intercepts), Nvidia's $115.84bn quarter (revenue doubled; board rejects
Huang's return; Trump backs him), the Gaza ceasefire warning (Board of
Peace; WFP: one hot meal every two days) and Voyager 1 nearing one
light-day from Earth. A ticker adds Dhoni's IPL retirement, Cyclone Akvan,
Latvia's new government and the UN's October Ukraine civilian toll.

The image is drawn entirely in code (`generate.py`, PIL primitives — no
photos, no downloaded art). `main.py` (Python standard library only)
copies the finished `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.

## Run

```sh
python3 generate.py            # redraw entry.png (optional)
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
